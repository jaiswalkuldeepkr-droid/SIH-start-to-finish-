Legal Metrology Verification Portal

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Government of India

Every stamp, verified in seconds.

Weighing scales, weighbridges, fuel dispensers and water meters across India must be tested and stamped before use. This portal moves that entire process online — registration, inspection, digital certification and public verification — in one place.

Try a sample: · ·
01

Register the instrument

Owners and dealers list each weighing or measuring instrument with its serial number, type and location, and receive a unique instrument ID.

02

Inspect and test

A Legal Metrology officer schedules a physical test against reference standards and records the accuracy readings against permitted error limits.

03

Certify and stamp digitally

A signed digital certificate with a QR code replaces the physical stamp. Anyone can scan or search it to confirm it's genuine and current.
